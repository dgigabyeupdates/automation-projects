## 📁 Project 1 — Automated Tax Status Management System

**Client:** University of Abuja
**Tool:** n8n
**Status:** ✅ Live & Published

### Overview
Built an end-to-end automation workflow to eliminate manual tax status processing for University of Abuja staff. The system runs entirely without human intervention from form submission through to personalised staff notification.

### How It Works
1. **Trigger** — Workflow activates automatically on form submission
2. **Google Sheets** — Staff tax record is appended or updated in real time
3. **Switch Node** — Conditional logic routes the workflow based on tax status
4. **Gmail** — Personalised notification email sent automatically to the staff member

### Results
- ✅ Zero manual data entry required
- ✅ Eliminated processing delays
- ✅ Every staff member receives accurate, timely tax status communication
- ✅ Full audit trail maintained in Google Sheets

### Tools Used
`n8n` `Google Sheets` `Gmail` `Form Trigger` `Switch/Router Node`

## 📁 Project 2 — Real-Time Customer Interest Email Notification System

**Client:** Business Client (Confidential)
**Tool:** Make (Integromat)
**Status:** ✅ Live & Published

### Overview
Built an automated monitoring and notification system that watches a Google Drive folder every 15 minutes for new customer interest signals, then instantly sends tailored Gmail notifications — ensuring no lead goes unactioned.

### How It Works
1. **Scheduled Trigger** — Make scenario runs automatically every 15 minutes
2. **Google Drive** — Monitors a designated folder for any new files
3. **Gmail** — Automatically sends a tailored notification email when a new file is detected

### Results
- ✅ Zero manual folder monitoring required
- ✅ Response delays eliminated — leads actioned in under 15 minutes
- ✅ Always-on system running 24/7 without supervision
- ✅ No leads missed regardless of time of day

### Tools Used
`Make (Integromat)` `Google Drive` `Gmail` `Scheduled Trigger` `Email Automation`
